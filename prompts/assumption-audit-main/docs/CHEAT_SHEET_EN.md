# Assumption Audit – Cheat Sheet (EN) (v1.0.3)

One-page quick reference.  
Goal: keep the audit an **alarm, not a verdict**.

---

## When to use it (fast trigger)

Use the audit when you hear:
- “must”, “can’t be otherwise”, “just technical”, “best practice”, “the process decided”, “the data shows”.

---

## Crisis skeleton (90 seconds)

1) **CLASSIFY**: A politics | B product/UX | C AI/algorithm | D mixed  
2) **RED FLAG** (1 sentence)  
3) **STATUS** (1 line)  
4) **CORES+** (6 lines)  
5) **3 trade-offs**  
6) **2 alternatives**  
7) **TIME (sunset)**  
8) **PRE-MORTEM** (tick-box / bypass / malicious compliance)  
9) **3 interventions**  
10) **2 publish-ready sentences**

---

## RED FLAG templates

- “Hidden assumption: **[X]** is presented as fact even though it is a choice.”  
- “Choice disguised as necessity: **[X]** (metric/default/accountability is hidden).”  
- “Process replaces justification: **[X]** — but alternatives and costs aren’t compared.”

---

## STATUS (anti-paranoia guardrail)

**Format (one line):**  
`STATUS: [Fact/Hypothesis/Question], confidence [low/medium/high], Evidence Tier [A/B/C], missing [minimum proof], stop rule: [what counts as enough].`

**Evidence Tier**
- **A** = primary source (law, decision, official report, raw data)
- **B** = credible secondary (reputable media, academic summary)
- **C** = signals only (inference without hard proof)

**No intent claims without evidence**  
Never state motive as fact. Motive = Hypothesis only + 1 benign alternative.

---

## CORES+ (6 lines)

1) **Authority**: who decided + who is accountable  
2) **Profit**: who benefits from the status quo/default  
3) **Metric**: what is being optimized  
4) **Default**: what is default + cost of opting out  
5) **Costs**: who bears them  
6) **Excluded alternative**: what was pushed out of scope

---

## Trade-off template (3 lines)

- “**X over Y**; cost borne by **Z**.”

X/Y examples:
- speed vs accuracy
- frictionless flow vs legitimacy
- revenue vs autonomy
- control vs privacy
- standardization vs plurality

---

## Alternatives (2 lines)

- Option 1: **[what]** (cost/risk: **[1 sentence]**)  
- Option 2: **[what]** (cost/risk: **[1 sentence]**)  
Tip: if rushed, do a **default switch** (“what if the default were the opposite?”).

---

## TIME (sunset) – mandatory question

- “When does it **automatically expire** unless benefits are proven?”  
- “What is the **minimum proof of benefit** (metric + threshold + deadline)?”

---

## PRE-MORTEM (3 bullets)

1) Tick-box: how does it become paper-only compliance?  
2) Bypass: how is it dodged in practice?  
3) Malicious compliance: what extreme by-the-book behavior destroys the goal?

---

## 3 interventions (ready-made)

- “Visible opt-out without penalties.”  
- “Mandatory metric justification + published alternatives.”  
- “Sunset deadline + independent audit + remedy path.”

---

## 2 publish-ready sentence templates

- “This isn’t necessity; it’s a choice—and alternatives aren’t compared.”  
- “Efficient by which metric—and at whose expense?”  
- “Who decides and who benefits are not the same.”

---

## 10-second mini checklist

- Hidden assumption?  
- Who benefits?  
- Which metric?  
- Which default and opt-out cost?  
- When does it end?
